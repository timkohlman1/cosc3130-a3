Introduction - 1 page (2 marks)

Briefly describe your topic and why it is important or interesting. You could start by describing the area of
interest and later summarise the existing solutions and their limitations. You could also explain the specific focus
of your research and why you are doing so. Feel free to include specific information, such as detailed statistics,
that will back up your claims (and thus make your statements much stronger).

In summary, an introduction should:

\begin{itemize}
\item Introduce the topic and its significance.
\item Give background and context.
\item Outline your problem and research questions.
\end{itemize}

Some important questions to guide your introduction include:
\begin{itemize}
\item Who is interested in the topic (e.g. scientists, practitioners, policymakers, particular members of society)?
\item How much is already known about the problem?
\item What is missing from current body knowledge?
\item What new insights will your research contribute?
\item Why is this research worth doing
\end{itemize}
