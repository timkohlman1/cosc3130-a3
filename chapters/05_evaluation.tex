\section{Evaluation Metrics}
For evaluation plan, the main objective is to determine how effective the proposed model is in detecting inauthentic social media post. To achieve this, standard performance metrics (https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/model-performance) are used to measure how the model identified between authentic and inauthentic post.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{1.} Accuracy, measures the number of correct classification divided by the total number of classifications.
    \item \textbf{2.} Recall, measures the number of inauthentic posts correctly classified out of all actual inauthentic posts (true positive rate or TPR).
    \item \textbf{3.} Precision, measures the number of inauthentic posts correctly classified out of all number of inauthentic post classified by the model.
    \item \textbf{4.} F1 Score, harmonic mean of recall and precision, considers the two measures to be of equal weight to balance out any false positives or false negatives.
\end{itemize}
These metrics are compared against the baseline models to assess the effectiveness of the proposed model \citep{XUE2021102610}.
A high accuracy means that a model is more accurate in classifying inauthentic social media posts.
High precision and high recall also indicating that the model ensures inauthentic post is detected and avoids incorrectly labelling authentic content as inauthentic.
F1 score will be used as the main comparison between model and experiment datasets as it capture the balance of detection capability (recall) and reliability (precision).
All metrics are evaluated across Stage 1 and Stage 2 (Conditions A–D).
This allows for a consistent comparison between the baseline and modified model, and helps to measure the contribution of multimodal content and social context features.
