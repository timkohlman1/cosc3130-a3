\subsection{Project Plan}
The scope of the project is only to focus on developing a research proposal that designs a context-aware multimodal misinformation detection framework with DANES/GETAE models as baselines.
The proposal will include an introduction of misinformation detection approaches, formulation of research questions, literature review on context-aware and multimodal framework on misinformation detection, proposed methodology involving modified DANES/GETAE models,
and evaluation metrics on model performance.

This project will not cover the full implementation of models proposed in the methodology section, model testing or performance benchmarking.
It also will not cover dataset collection or processing, nor model deployment.

The project team will adopt an Agile-Scrum approach to ensure clear task allocation, accountability and timely development of the research proposal.
Each member of the team will have responsibility to deliver section(s) of proposal.
Project work will be organised into weekly sprints where regular team meeting will be held for sprint planning, progress update and sprint review to resolve any problem or progress blockers.

Several tools will be used to support collaborative work and project management, such as:
(1) Microsoft Teams for team communications and task distributions,
(2) GitHub for version control and tracking proposal document changes, and
(3) Overleaf for collaborative writing and integration of proposal document.
These tools enable efficient project progress tracking and team communication throughout the project.

\subsection{Risk and Mitigation}
The project adopts a proactive risk management approach by identifying potential risks that may impact research proposal outcome and timeline.
Key risks identified include unclear research direction, complex methodology design, and challenges in selecting suitable datasets.
To resolve these risks, we are defining mitigation and contingency strategies to minimise disruption to project progress.
Any unresolved or newly identified risks will be escalated to Project Leader and discussed in weekly meetings to determine response and contingency actions. Refer to appendix page for detailed risks and mitigation plan.
