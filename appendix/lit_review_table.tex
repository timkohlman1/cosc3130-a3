\textbf{\citet{Wang2018} -- EANN} \newline
Early multimodal fake news detection
&
Showed that combining text and image features improves fake news detection compared with text-only or image-only models. It established an early two-branch multimodal structure for fake news detection.
&
Fusion was still relatively early-stage and did not fully model deeper cross-modal relationships. It also did not include a DANES/GETAE-style propagation branch. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Nakamura2020} -- Fakeddit} \newline
Multimodal benchmark dataset
&
Provided a large-scale multimodal benchmark with text, images, metadata, comments, and multiple fake news labels. It showed that multimodal classifiers outperform unimodal baselines.
&
Focused more on benchmarking than controlled integration of multimodal content with propagation-based social context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{shu2020fakenewsnet} -- FakeNewsNet} \newline
Dataset for fake news research
&
Provided news content, social context, and spatiotemporal information through PolitiFact and GossipCop. It is relevant as a candidate dataset for multimodal and context-aware evaluation.
&
Practical use may be limited by missing media, removed posts, incomplete social traces, and preprocessing requirements. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Xue2021} -- MCNN} \newline
Text-image consistency modelling
&
Demonstrated that modelling the relationship between text and image improves fake news classification, especially when visual evidence conflicts with textual claims.
&
Mainly content-focused and does not include propagation-based social context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Qian2021} -- HMCAN} \newline
Hierarchical multimodal attention
&
Introduced hierarchical multimodal contextual attention using BERT and ResNet-based feature extraction. It is directly relevant because MACE uses HMCAN as the selected multimodal content branch.
&
Strong fit for the content branch, but HMCAN alone remains content-focused and does not model DANES/GETAE-style propagation context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Song2021}} \newline
Temporally evolving graph neural network
&
Showed that temporal diffusion patterns and evolving graph structures provide useful evidence for fake news detection.
&
Relies on interaction data, which may be sparse, delayed, or unavailable in early detection and some datasets. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Min2022}} \newline
Post-user interaction network
&
Demonstrated that modelling post-user interactions through graph structures can improve fake news detection.
&
Less effective when user activity data is limited, missing, or not transferable across datasets. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{mumindataset} -- MuMin} \newline
Multilingual multimodal dataset
&
Provided a large-scale multilingual multimodal misinformation dataset with tweets, users, claims, and graph relationships. It is highly relevant for RQ3 and dataset adaptation.
&
Its scale, multilingual nature, and graph complexity create preprocessing, storage, and computational feasibility challenges. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Segura2022}} \newline
Multimodal fake news benchmarking
&
Reinforced evidence that multimodal approaches outperform text-only models on social media misinformation tasks.
&
Does not resolve how multimodality performs when propagation-based social context is held constant. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Jing2023} -- MPFN} \newline
Progressive multimodal fusion
&
Demonstrated that better fusion design, not just adding more modalities, improves multimodal fake news detection.
&
Useful as related work, but MPFN is not selected for MACE and has limited integration with DANES/GETAE-style social context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Nadeem2023} -- EFND} \newline
Semantic, visual, and social features
&
Showed that semantic, visual, and socially augmented features can produce strong detection performance when fused together.
&
Social context differs from DANES/GETAE propagation graphs, making it hard to isolate whether gains come from multimodality or added context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{truica2024danes} -- DANES} \newline
Context-aware ensemble model
&
Combined a text-based content branch with a social-context branch and achieved strong benchmark performance. It is one of the primary baselines for MACE.
&
The content branch remains text-only, so visual misinformation may be missed. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Wang2024}} \newline
Comment-context masked transformer network
&
Supported the value of combining multimodal content with comment-based social context and filtering noisy content or comments.
&
Comment-context representation differs from DANES/GETAE propagation branches, making direct comparison difficult. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Szu-Yin24}} \newline
Text-image multimodal fusion
&
Compared early, joint, and late fusion strategies using BERT and CNN-based image features, showing that fusion design affects performance.
&
Useful for fusion discussion, but mainly focuses on content-level multimodal fusion rather than retained propagation context. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Zhu24}} \newline
Propagation structure-aware graph transformer
&
Showed that propagation structure can improve robustness and interpretability in fake news detection.
&
Performance depends on the availability and completeness of network or interaction data. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Baltrusaitis2019}} \newline
Multimodal machine learning survey
&
Provides a conceptual foundation for multimodal fusion, alignment, and representation learning. This is useful for explaining why fusion design matters.
&
General multimodal learning survey rather than a fake news-specific model. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Devlin2019} -- BERT} \newline
Transformer text encoder
&
Established BERT as a strong transformer-based text encoder. It supports the use of BERT in HMCAN and cost estimation for the text branch.
&
Not specific to fake news detection and introduces substantial parameter and fine-tuning cost. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{He2016} -- ResNet} \newline
Image feature extraction
&
Introduced ResNet, including ResNet-50, which is widely used for image feature extraction and is part of the HMCAN-style visual pipeline.
&
Adds visual feature extraction cost compared with text-only models and may be less semantically aligned than newer CLIP or ViT-based encoders. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{GETAE2025} -- GETAE} \newline
Graph-enhanced ensemble model
&
Extended DANES by incorporating graph information and propagation-enhanced embeddings. It is the second primary baseline for MACE.
&
Still inherits the weakness of a text-only content branch. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{jing2025} -- DPSG} \newline
Dynamic propagation social graphs
&
Combined multimodal content with dynamic propagation social graphs and reported strong performance. It is one of the closest related works to MACE.
&
Changes both the multimodal and social graph components, so the exact source of improvement is unclear. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Dellys2025}} \newline
Multimodal fusion with social context
&
Demonstrated that incorporating social context can improve multimodal fake news detection.
&
Does not fully isolate multimodal substitution within a DANES/GETAE-style architecture. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Chen2025} -- TMEF-BI} \newline
Trusted multimodal evidential fusion
&
Introduced uncertainty-aware and reliability-based multimodal fusion with behaviour information.
&
Behaviour information differs from propagation-graph social context, limiting direct transfer to MACE. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Sun25} -- ESDGFN} \newline
Dynamic graph and evolving stance
&
Integrated multimodal source content, dynamic conversation graphs, and evolving user stance.
&
Adds architectural complexity and reduces comparability with simpler DANES/GETAE baselines. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Hao24}} \newline
Self-learning multimodal fake news detection
&
Explored contrastive learning and LLM-supported multimodal analysis to address limited labelled data.
&
Relevant as an emerging method, but less directly aligned with the controlled DANES/GETAE substitution design. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Chiang2019} -- Cluster-GCN} \newline
Large graph learning
&
Showed that large-scale graph learning can require substantial memory and may need graph partitioning or mini-batch strategies. This is useful for assessing MuMin feasibility.
&
Not a fake news detection paper, but relevant for graph scalability and computational feasibility. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Mattson2020} -- MLPerf} \newline
Machine learning benchmarking
&
Provides a standard approach for reporting compute performance, including wall-clock time and hardware configuration.
&
General benchmarking framework, not specific to misinformation detection. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Radford2021} and \citet{Dosovitskiy2021}} \newline
CLIP and ViT-based visual encoders
&
Show that newer vision-language or transformer-based visual encoders can improve image-text alignment compared with standard CNN encoders.
&
Often increase computational cost and change the content branch more substantially, making controlled comparison with HMCAN harder. \\

\midrule

\textbf{\citet{Ji2026LDPG} -- LDPG} \newline
Local dynamic propagation graph-based multimodal fake news detection
&
Further supports the effectiveness of combining multimodal content with propagation-aware social context, especially through local dynamic graph modelling.
&
The separate effect of multimodality is still not clearly decomposed because the model changes multiple components together. \\
